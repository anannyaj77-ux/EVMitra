EV VIDEO PLACEHOLDER
=====================

To show an EV video on the EVMitra Home page:

1. Obtain a royalty-free EV video (e.g., from Pexels.com → search "electric vehicle")
2. Rename it to: ev_showcase.mp4
3. Place it in this folder: EVMitra/assets/ev_showcase.mp4
4. Restart the Streamlit app

The app will automatically detect the video and display it.

If the file is not present, a YouTube embed is shown as fallback (requires internet).

Suggested free sources:
- https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/electric%20vehicle/
- https://pixabay.com/videos/search/electric%20car/

Note: Ensure you have rights to use any video in your demonstration.
